# Propensity Analysis


## Overview

In this verison of the project, it is comparing different effects of students who got a particular aid type with similar students who have not recieved that particular aid type. This would answer our final question on "does this aid type influence student success", => IMPACT.

The previous version: What students were at risk? which is not our question.

---

## Propensity Analysis Process
1- Treatment: students who got the aid type e.g. institutional aid in year 1.

2- Comparison (Treatment): students who didn't get it but recieved other aid that year.

3- Make them comparable: match on similar studens like their (GPA, test scores, earlier aid), so the two groups start out alike.

4- Compare outcomes: retention and graduation rates between the matched groups. The difference is the estimated effect of that aid type.


---

## Limitations

1. It only adjusts for what we measured.
  Like many socio-economic factors are not measured, thus need based aid (federal and state) may be biased.

2. It only compares aid types, not aid vs. no aid. Basically, since our data only has aid recipients we only compare between other aid, not "aid compared with nothing".

3. Results only cover students who could be matched. If any treated students have no similarity, the estimate doesn't represent all the population.
